# Centro de Ciencia de Datos e Inteligencia Artificial

**Data science, machine learning and operations research for public policy and
social good** across Mexico and Latin America.

> Escuela de Gobierno y Transformación Pública · Tecnológico de Monterrey

Our open-source work lives here — start with
**[featurizer](https://github.com/ccd-ia/featurizer)**.

---

## What we do

- **Temporal ML** for prioritizing and targeting public programs, with
  point-in-time correctness (no label leakage).
- **Optimization / operations research** for logistics, territorial coverage
  and resource allocation.
- **Computer vision and applied AI.**
- **Training and capacity transfer** to government teams.

> **Guiding principle:** objective functions encode political choices. We
> document what is optimized, for whom, and the trade-offs between efficiency
> and equity.
